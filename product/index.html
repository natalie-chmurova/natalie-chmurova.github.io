From product conversations to delivery-ready work

A meeting ends.
The work is ready.

A practical tool that turns raw meeting transcripts — or audio recordings — into ClickUp-ready tasks, sprint summaries and bug-triage tables. An agentic n8n workflow scores its own confidence, routes doubtful calls to Telegram for one-tap approval, keeps a live ClickUp board in sync with zero duplicates, assigns owners from spoken names, and is safe to re-run.

What it does +
  • Agentic workflow with human-in-the-loop — Telegram Approve/Reject before anything is written
  • Create-or-update board sync — new context as comments, only genuinely new tasks, zero duplicates
  • Deterministic idempotency + owner-assignment — safe re-runs, tasks land on the right person
  • Two eval harnesses (LLM-as-judge) + decision log — prompts tuned against numbers, decisions logged for review
