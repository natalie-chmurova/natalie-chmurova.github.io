I made a video about the AI tools I use for different jobs ⭐️ Here’s the full list with links.

✍️ Writing

  • 👇 SKIP — DeepSeek (deepseek.com) — smart and almost free, but the style is flat
  • ✨ FINE — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) — a generalist that knows a bit of everything
  • 🔥 LOVE — Claude (claude.ai) — keeps your tone and doesn’t slide into clichés. I write everything in it

📌 Fact-checking

  • 👇 SKIP — Google — ten tabs, and you sort it out yourself
  • ✨ FINE — ChatGPT — answers beautifully, but can make things up
  • 🔥 LOVE — Perplexity (perplexity.ai) — gives you sources for every answer

🧩 Generating video

  • 👇 SKIP — Sora — shut down back in April, and on September 24 developer access was switched off too: it burned about a million dollars a day and made only two million over its whole life :(
  • ✨ FINE — Runway (runway.com) — lots of control, but pricey
  • 🔥 LOVE — Veo — video with sound straight away. It has no app of its own and lives inside Gemini (gemini.google.com), which is why I list it under Gemini.

A cheaper alternative is Kling (kling.ai).

📹 Editing video

  • 👇 SKIP — CapCut — by hand, the whole evening gone
  • ✨ FINE — Descript (descript.com) — delete a word in the transcript and it disappears from the video
  • 🔥 LOVE — Claude + Remotion (remotion.dev) — this is how I edit my reels. Remotion builds video out of code, and Claude writes that code from my plain-words description. Remotion has no app, so the two work as a pair

🤩 Making sense of it all

You can pay $300 for a course. You can spend three hours on YouTube. Or you can follow me on Instagram and YouTube and get into AI a little at a time 💻
