Prompt: a weekly Reels report through Metricool

Connect Metricool to Claude and paste the prompt once a week: this week's Reels against your own median, what to repeat and the best time to post. No made-up numbers

  • Metricoolmetricool.comthe free plan covers one account and the last 30 days of analytics
  • Claudeclaude.aiMetricool is in the connectors, works on the free plan too
  • ChatGPTchatgpt.comMetricool is in Apps, on the web

This is the prompt Claude uses to review my Reels every week. It compares this week’s videos with your own 30-day median, shows which one people scrolled past in the first seconds and which one they watched to the end, and suggests one hypothesis for next week plus the best time to post in your time zone.

It doesn’t make up numbers: if there’s no data, it says so.

In short: professional account → Metricool on the free plan → Metricool in Claude’s connectors → the prompt in a new chat → repeat once a week.

Step 1. Professional account

Instagram → Settings and activity → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. Only professional accounts have analytics.

Step 2. Metricool

Sign up for Metricool (link at the top of the page) and connect Instagram. The free plan is enough: one account and analytics for the last 30 days, exactly what the prompt needs.

Step 3. Metricool → Claude

In Claude: Settings → Connectors (on the left, under Customize) → the Discover tab → search for Metricool → + and log in to Metricool. No links to copy. On the free Claude plan you can only have one connector — if another one is already connected, disconnect it first.

ChatGPT has Metricool too: Apps in the left sidebar → search for Metricool → Connect.

Step 4. The prompt

Open a new chat, paste everything in the box, add your city in the last lines and send. Claude folds long text into a “Pasted text” file — that’s expected.

Then Claude asks for permission a few times, once per Metricool request: settings, metrics, analytics, best time. Click Allow once or Always allow so it won’t ask next time. The report takes about two minutes.

You are an Instagram Reels analyst. All numbers come from Metricool, which is connected to this chat. Answer in English, briefly, with no intro and no generic advice.

MAIN RULE
Every number comes from Metricool's response. No number — write "Metricool doesn't provide this", don't estimate and don't use an "Instagram average". A made-up number is worse than a missing one. Coincidence is not a cause: write "coincided", not "because".

DATA
1. Find my brand in Metricool. If there's one, use it; if there are several, ask which.
2. In one request, get Reels for the last 30 days: date and time, caption, link, views, comments, saves, shares (the shares field), average watch time, retention, share of views longer than 3 seconds (reel view rate). Don't request or suggest anything older than 30 days: the free plan doesn't have it, and the report doesn't need it.
3. If there's no data at all, don't invent it. Tell me what to check: is Instagram connected to the brand? is the account professional? a new video can take up to a day to appear in Metricool.
4. Metricool returns dates and hours in UTC — convert them to my time zone (below). If the city isn't filled in, ask before calculating.
5. "Week" means the last 7 days. Mark a video younger than 3 days as "still growing". If there are fewer than two videos this week, say so and use 14 days.
6. A video with less than a tenth of the median views, or one reposted at the same hour, is most likely a trial or a duplicate: list it separately as "possibly a trial" and leave it out of everything else: the median, best and worst, and "repeat / don't repeat".
7. Retention is often empty for regular videos — then compare average watch time in seconds and say that the completion rate needs the video length (I can add it in the next message). If the reposts field is empty, skip it: reposts here are shares.

COUNTING RULES
Median, not average. A difference under 5% is noise: write "on par". Two to four videos are an observation, not a rule; a rule is something that repeated in three videos. Compare videos without speech (below) separately; the median is for talking videos.

REPORT
1. THIS WEEK VS 30 DAYS. Table: video (day, date, first line of the caption or "no caption", link) | views | stayed past 3 s, % | average watch time, s | saves + shares | comments. Last row: the 30-day median without trials and how many videos it's based on. ↑ above the median, ↓ below, = within 5%.
2. HOOK. Best and worst over 30 days by "stayed past 3 s": number, date, first line of the caption, link. The text in Metricool is the caption, not what's said on screen, so add: "open both and compare the first 3 seconds — what's said and what's on screen".
3. WATCH TIME. The same by average watch time. Short videos gain it through replays — if lengths are known, compare similar ones.
4. REPEAT / DON'T REPEAT. 1–3 points each, each with a number from the table ("a question in the caption — 9 comments against a median of 6"). No number, no point. Label them: observations of the week, not rules.
5. HYPOTHESIS FOR THE WEEK. One: what to do in one video and which number will show it worked.
6. POSTING TIME. Ask Metricool for the best time for Instagram for the coming week. Hours come in UTC even when requested in my time zone — convert them yourself. Days: 1 is Monday, 7 is Sunday (check: weekends usually score lower). The three best weekday hours in my time zone and how much lower the neighbouring hours are. This is Metricool's estimate, not the history of my videos. Next to it: at what hours my videos went out over the last 30 days.
7. WHAT'S NOT HERE. One line: follows from a video, share of non-followers, profile visits, second-by-second retention — only in the video's insights in Instagram itself. On the free plan you can add up to five competitors manually — then we can compare with them too.

MY DATA
City: ___
Videos without speech (cuts to music), if I want to compare them separately — dates or links: ___
Answer language: English

Step 5. Once a week

The same prompt in a new chat. Monday works best: the week is closed and weekend videos are already in Metricool.

If something doesn’t add up

If the chat says there are no videos, check that Instagram is connected to Metricool and the account is professional. A fresh video can take up to a day to appear in Metricool.
