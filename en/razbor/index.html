01

What it is

This is not a lecture or a course. You come with one task that takes up your time every week: editing a video, going through your analytics, writing a draft, putting together a report.

Together we set up the AI to do this task by your rules: your files, your style, your constraints. At the end of the meeting, you repeat it all yourself on a new example.

02

What people come with

Three tasks I’ve already handed over to AI myself. Yours can be anything.

editing

Editing by my own rules

My talking-head videos are edited by AI: pauses, looking away, subtitles, sound. Writing the rules took a month.

analytics

Analytics without paid tools

An Instagram data export and one prompt instead of a paid analytics service.

method

A prompt that fits the task

Context, task, criteria: the template I use to give AI any task.

For work or for your blog: drafts, reports, spreadsheets, emails. If your task isn’t on the list, write to me and I’ll tell you whether it fits into one meeting.

03

How it works

  1. 1

    Before the meeting: a short chat.

    You tell me what you want to simplify and what you already use. I tell you what we can realistically do in one meeting, and we agree on the result.

  2. 2

    The meeting: 90 minutes online.

    Using your own material: we look at how you do the task now, write instructions for the AI and get to the first result.

  3. 3

    At the end: you do it yourself.

    You run it again on a new example, without me. That’s how we both know it works without me.

05

What it isn’t

To be upfront

  • I won’t teach you to code. You don’t need to.
  • I won’t sell you a course: I don’t have one.
  • We won’t cover every AI tool in one meeting: just your one task, start to finish.
  • No passwords, payment details or other people’s materials in the meeting: we work on your examples, and you type in any logins yourself.

Common questions

Do I need a computer?+

For most tasks, yes: a phone isn’t enough. I’ll tell you what kind once I’ve seen the task.

I’ve only ever used a chatbot. Is this for me?+

Yes. That is usually where people start. The point of the session is for the chat to stop being “ask and forget”.

What if we don’t finish in one meeting?+

If the task doesn’t fit into one meeting, I’ll tell you in our chat, before you pay. If we only find out during the meeting, we’ll set up a second one.

Is it a recorded course or a live meeting?+

Live, just the two of us, with screen sharing. No recordings and no ready-made lessons.
